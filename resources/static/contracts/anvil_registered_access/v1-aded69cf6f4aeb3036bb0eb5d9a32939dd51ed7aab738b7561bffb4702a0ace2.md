AnVIL Registered-Access User Agreement (DRAFT)
1. Eligibility and Identity
1.1 Qualifying categories. To register for AnVIL registered-access datasets, an individual must fall into one of the following categories:
•	Bona fide researcher. A person confirmed by their institution as engaged in legitimate scientific research, via institutional SSO, an attestation letter, or vouching by another confirmed registered user. [3]
•	Clinical care professional. A licensed clinician (physician, genetic counselor, nurse practitioner, or equivalent) confirmed by their institution or a valid professional license. [3]
1.2 Institutional affiliation requirement. Users must register with an institutional email address from a recognized research institution, university, medical center, or equivalent. Personal webmail (e.g., gmail.com) is not accepted. [3,8]
1.3 Account linking. Users must link their AnVIL/Terra account to a verified identity credential. U.S.-based researchers must connect a Login.gov or ID.me account to NIH eRA Commons via NIH RAS. International researchers follow AnVIL's designated equivalent pathway. [1,5]
2. Attestation
By accepting this Agreement, the Registered User makes the following attestations:
1.	Identity and role. I am the person I claim to be. My institutional or organizational affiliation is current and accurate. I am a bona fide researcher or licensed clinical care professional as described in Section 1. [3]
2.	Legitimate research purpose. I will access registered-access datasets solely for scientific research or clinical purposes that are consistent with the informed consent under which the data were collected, the stated data use limitations of each dataset, and applicable ethical and legal requirements. [1,2]
3.	No commercial exploitation without approval. I will not use registered-access datasets for commercial product development or other commercial purposes unless explicitly permitted by the dataset's terms. [2,4]
4.	Non-re-identification. I will not attempt to re-identify any research participant, directly or indirectly, without prior written permission from the submitting institution. [2]
5.	Compliance with consent. I will respect the consent-based data use permissions and restrictions associated with each dataset, including any GA4GH Consent Codes or Data Use Ontology terms attached to the data. [1,4]
6.	Data security. I will implement reasonable security measures to protect data against unauthorized access, as described in Section 4. [2]
7.	Accuracy. My registration information is accurate and complete. I will promptly update it if material information changes. [3]
8.	Training. I have completed or will complete, prior to data access, any ethics or responsible conduct of research training required by AnVIL or applicable NIH policy. [1]
3. Responsible Data Use
3.1 Permitted uses. The Registered User may use registered-access datasets to: [1,2]
•	Conduct scientific research consistent with the consent terms and data use limitations of the specific dataset.
•	Develop and test computational methods and analysis pipelines, provided results are used for research purposes and not to re-identify participants.
•	Collaborate with other Registered Users or Approved Users (under controlled access) on joint research projects, provided each collaborator independently holds the appropriate access level for the data they use.
•	Publish or publicly present research findings derived from the data, subject to Section 6 (acknowledgment requirements).
3.2 Prohibited uses. The Registered User may not: [1,2]
•	Attempt to identify, contact, or locate individual research participants, or generate information that could make the identity of a participant readily ascertainable, except with explicit prior written permission from the submitting institution.
•	Share, distribute, transfer, or disclose registered-access datasets, or derivatives that could be used to re-identify participants, to any person who has not independently accepted a registered-access or controlled-access agreement for those datasets.
•	Download data to unsecured local environments or transmit data outside of AnVIL-approved cloud computing environments unless AnVIL policy at the time of access explicitly permits local download.
•	Apply for, or assist others in applying for, intellectual property protection over genomic sequences or variants derived from registered-access data, except where the specific dataset's terms of use explicitly permit this.
3.3 Cloud environment. Users should analyze data within AnVIL/Terra; those who download to local systems bear sole responsibility for meeting Section 4 security requirements, applicable regulations, and any egress costs. [7,8]
4. Security Obligations
4.1 General standard. Users must implement reasonable technical and administrative safeguards against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of registered-access data, consistent with NIH Security Best Practices for Users of Controlled-Access Data. [2,9]
4.2 Specific requirements.
•	Data stored outside the AnVIL/Terra cloud environment must be encrypted at rest and in transit using current, widely accepted encryption standards.
•	Access to data files, decryption keys, and analysis environments must be restricted to the Registered User and any authorized collaborators who have independently accepted a registered-access or controlled-access agreement for those datasets. Credentials must not be shared.
•	Multi-factor authentication must be enabled on any account used to access AnVIL, Terra, or any system holding registered-access data.
•	Devices used to access or store registered-access data must have operating system and security software kept up to date.
4.3 Cloud-first expectation. Where feasible, analyze data within Terra to maintain AnVIL's FISMA Moderate security perimeter. [7]
4.4 Third-party services. Users who process registered-access data in third-party services remain solely responsible for meeting the security standards in this Section. [1,2]
5. Privacy and Participant Protections
5.1 Participant privacy. Registered-access datasets may contain sensitive genomic, phenotypic, or health-related information. Users will treat this data with the same care as identifiable human subjects data, regardless of formal de-identification status. [1,2]
5.2 Non-re-identification. Users will not link or combine registered-access data with any other dataset to identify, directly or indirectly, individual participants. [1,2]
5.3 Consent compliance. Users will review and comply with each dataset's consent-based use limitations (GA4GH Consent Codes, DUO terms, or free-text restrictions). When limitations are ambiguous, contact AnVIL data stewards before proceeding. [1,4]
5.4 Certificate of Confidentiality. For datasets covered by an NIH Certificate of Confidentiality (NOT-OD-17-109), users may not disclose identifiable information in any legal or administrative proceeding except as the Certificate permits. [6]
5.5 No participant contact. The Registered User will not use registered-access data to initiate direct contact with research participants, unless the submitting institution has granted explicit prior written permission for recontact for a specified research purpose. [1]
6. Acknowledgment and Publication
6.1 Attribution. Publications and presentations must cite the dataset name and accession number (dbGaP accession or AnVIL Dataset ID), acknowledge the investigators and institutions that generated the data, and credit the funding NIH Institute or Center as specified in the dataset record, including grant numbers where required. [1,2]
6.2 Platform acknowledgment. Publications using AnVIL infrastructure or tools should acknowledge the AnVIL platform. [8] Suggested language: "This research was conducted using the NHGRI Analysis, Visualization, and Informatics Lab-space (AnVIL) at https://anvilproject.org."
6.3 Pre-publication sharing. Pre-publication findings that could reveal participant data must not be shared without notifying the data submitter and following any embargo requirements in the dataset record. [1]
7. Reporting Obligations
7.1 Mandatory reporting. The Registered User must promptly report any of the following to AnVIL data stewards (anvil-data-access@broadinstitute.org or the contact designated by AnVIL at the time of access): [1,2]
•	Any inadvertent disclosure of registered-access data to an unauthorized party.
•	Any suspected or confirmed breach of data security (e.g., unauthorized access, loss of a device containing registered-access data, ransomware, or similar incident).
•	Any awareness that the data has been or may have been used in a manner inconsistent with this Agreement.
7.2 Reporting timeline. Reports must be made within 24 hours of becoming aware of a potential or confirmed incident; a written account must follow within 5 business days. [1]
7.3 Cooperation and evidence preservation. Users will cooperate fully with any investigation by AnVIL, NHGRI, NIH, or the relevant Data Access Committee, provide relevant logs and records on request, and will not conceal, delete, or alter records related to a suspected incident. [1]
8. Renewal and Recertification
8.1 Term and renewal. This Agreement takes effect on the date of acceptance, remains in effect for one year, and must be renewed annually by re-attesting and confirming current institutional affiliation and account linking via the AnVIL/Terra portal. [1,3]
8.2 NIH RAS recertification alignment. For users with NIH RAS identity linkage, annual recertification aligns with the NIH RAS IAL2 cycle. Failure to complete NIH RAS recertification suspends access regardless of this Agreement's status. [5]
8.3 Lapse of access. Failure to renew before expiration automatically suspends access. Users may re-register subject to current eligibility requirements. Data obligations under this Agreement survive suspension or expiration. [1]
8.4 Change in eligibility. Users must notify AnVIL within 30 days if their institutional affiliation ends, license lapses, or eligibility otherwise changes, and must not access data until eligibility is re-established. [1]
9. Enforcement and Sanctions
9.1 Monitoring and audit. AnVIL and NHGRI reserve the right to audit access logs, activity records, and compliance with the terms of this Agreement at any time. Use of AnVIL systems constitutes consent to such monitoring. [1,8]
9.2 Suspension. AnVIL may immediately suspend a Registered User's access pending investigation upon reasonable suspicion of a breach of this Agreement, a violation of applicable law, or a threat to participant privacy or platform integrity. Suspension does not constitute a final determination of wrongdoing.
9.3 Termination. Upon a finding of material breach, AnVIL or NHGRI may permanently terminate the Registered User's access. Grounds for termination include, but are not limited to: (a) attempting to re-identify participants; (b) unauthorized sharing or redistribution of registered-access data; (c) failure to report a known or suspected data breach; (d) misrepresentation of identity, institutional affiliation, or intended use; (e) use of data for purposes materially inconsistent with the stated purpose and the applicable dataset's consent terms. [1]
9.4 Referral. Serious or willful violations may be referred to the Registered User's institution, relevant professional licensing boards, NIH's Office of Research Integrity, or other appropriate governmental or regulatory bodies. [1]
9.5 Survival of data obligations. Data-handling, security, and reporting obligations survive termination or suspension for all data already accessed. [1]
9.6 Appeals. A Registered User whose access has been suspended or terminated may submit a written appeal to anvil-data-access@broadinstitute.org within 30 calendar days of receiving notice. Appeals will be reviewed by NHGRI or its designated representative, whose decision is final.
10. Governing Policies
10.1 Governing documents. This Agreement is governed by and interpreted consistent with the NIH GDS Policy [1], NIH Genomic Data User Code of Conduct [2], GA4GH Registered Access Framework [3], and AnVIL Terms of Service [8]. In the event of a conflict regarding data-access obligations, this Agreement governs.
10.2 Applicable law. U.S. federal law governs, including FISMA [7] and applicable provisions of HIPAA and the Common Rule [10].
10.3 Dataset-specific consent terms. Per-dataset DUO [4] restrictions supplement this Agreement; the more restrictive obligation applies.
10.4 Policy updates. NHGRI may update governing policies. Continued use following notice of a material change constitutes acceptance.
